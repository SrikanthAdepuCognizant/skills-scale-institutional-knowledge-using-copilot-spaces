# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a lightweight lifecycle: initiate work by validating the business need and aligning stakeholders, then plan scope, milestones, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. Teams execute in small increments, tracking work on a project board and using reviews, testing, and clear escalation paths to maintain quality and delivery visibility.

Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers set outcomes and priorities; Developers and QA validate and deliver the work; stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular team syncs, demos, and stakeholder updates keep people aligned, while risk registers and defined escalation paths make blockers and decisions visible.

Before release, teams meet acceptance criteria, pass CI and security checks, prepare release notes and rollback plans, and verify deployments. After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, retrospectives turn lessons into owned, trackable improvements. The process documents below provide details, templates, and checklists for each stage.
